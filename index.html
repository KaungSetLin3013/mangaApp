  1. Click "Open folder" → select your manga root folder
  2. Folder names are parsed automatically:

  3. Bracket groups: () ignored · [Artist] · [Language]
  4. Search by title, artist, or language in the search box
  5. Click ⓘ on any card to see full details
  6. ★ Star manga to add to Favourites · progress auto-saved
